IT Service Agreement

This IT Service Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into between Example Municipal Utilities Ltd., Harbour Road 12, 20457 Hamburg (the "Customer"), and Sample Managed Services GmbH, Industrial Park 4, 01067 Dresden (the "Provider"), each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

  1. Definitions

    1. "Services" means the managed IT services described in Annex A, including operation, monitoring, maintenance and support of the systems listed there.
    2. "Service Levels" means the availability, response and resolution targets specified in Annex A, section A.3.
    3. "Incident" means any event that is not part of the standard operation of a service and that causes, or may cause, an interruption to or a reduction in the quality of that service.
    4. "Change" means the addition, modification or removal of anything that could have an effect on the Services, including configuration items, software releases and operating procedures.
    5. "Customer Data" means all data, including personal data, that the Customer or its users provide to the Provider or that the Provider processes on behalf of the Customer in performing the Services.
    6. "Business Day" means Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in the federal state of Hamburg; "Service Hours" means 07:00 to 19:00 on Business Days, unless Annex A specifies otherwise for a particular service.
    7. "Service Credit" means the reduction of the monthly fee calculated in accordance with Annex A, section A.4, where a Service Level is not met.

  2. Subject matter

    1. The Provider shall provide the Services to the Customer in accordance with this Agreement and its Annexes. In the event of contradictions the following order of precedence applies: (a) Annex C (data processing), (b) the main body of this Agreement, (c) Annex A, (d) Annex B.
    2. The Services are provided from data centres located in the European Union. A relocation of service provision to a location outside the European Union requires the prior written consent of the Customer.
    3. The Provider shall perform the Services with the care of a prudent business person, in accordance with the state of the art and the recognised rules of good IT practice, in particular ITIL 4 and ISO/IEC 20000-1.
    4. The Customer may request additional services. Additional services are agreed in writing in the form of a change request and are remunerated according to the hourly rates in Annex B unless a fixed price is agreed.

  3. Transition

    1. The Provider shall take over the Services from the current service provider in a transition phase from 1 October 2026 to 31 December 2026 in accordance with the transition plan agreed between the Parties.
    2. The transition is completed when the Provider has demonstrated, in an acceptance test, that all services listed in Annex A are operated according to the Service Levels for a continuous period of ten Business Days.
    3. Service Credits do not apply during the transition phase. The Provider shall nevertheless report the achieved service levels weekly.
    4. The Customer shall provide the documentation, access rights and contacts reasonably required for the transition and shall instruct the current service provider to cooperate.

  4. Service levels and reporting

    1. The Provider shall meet the Service Levels set out in Annex A. Availability is measured monthly at the service access points defined there, excluding agreed maintenance windows.
    2. The Provider shall submit a service report by the fifth Business Day of each month. The report contains at least the achieved availability per service, the number of incidents by priority, response and resolution times, changes carried out and open risks.
    3. If a Service Level is missed in two consecutive months or in three months within a period of twelve months, the Provider shall present an improvement plan within ten Business Days and implement it after approval by the Customer.
    4. Service Credits are the Customer's exclusive financial remedy for missed Service Levels, without prejudice to the right of termination for cause under clause 14 and to claims for damages for wilful misconduct or gross negligence.

  5. Incident and change management

    1. The Provider shall operate a service desk that can be reached by telephone, e-mail and through a web portal during the Service Hours and, for incidents of priority 1, around the clock.
    2. Incidents are classified according to the priority matrix in Annex A, section A.3. The Customer may raise the priority of an incident at any time with reasons; the Provider shall follow the Customer's classification.
    3. Changes are carried out exclusively in accordance with the change management process. Standard changes listed in the service catalogue may be implemented without individual approval; all other changes require the approval of the Customer's change advisory board.
    4. Emergency changes required to restore a service may be carried out without prior approval. The Provider shall document them and submit them for retrospective approval at the next meeting of the change advisory board.

  6. Cooperation of the Customer

    1. The Customer shall name a contract manager and a deputy who are authorised to make and receive declarations relating to this Agreement.
    2. The Customer shall grant the Provider the access to premises, systems and information necessary to provide the Services and shall inform the Provider without delay of any changes in its IT environment that may affect the Services.
    3. If the Customer does not meet its obligations to cooperate, the Provider is released from its performance obligations to the extent that the failure prevents performance, provided that the Provider has notified the Customer in writing without undue delay.

  7. Personnel and subcontractors

    1. The Provider shall deploy only qualified and reliable personnel. Key personnel named in Annex A may only be replaced with the prior consent of the Customer, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.
    2. The Provider may use subcontractors only with the prior written consent of the Customer. The subcontractors listed in Annex C are deemed approved. The Provider remains responsible for the performance of its subcontractors as for its own.
    3. The Provider shall ensure that its personnel and subcontractors comply with the Customer's site rules and information security requirements, in particular the Information Security Policy POL-2026-0042 in its current version.

  8. Fees and payment

    1. The Customer shall pay the fees set out in Annex B. The monthly flat fees are invoiced monthly in arrears; services remunerated on a time and material basis are invoiced monthly together with the time sheets approved by the Customer.
    2. Invoices are payable within thirty days of receipt of a correct invoice. Invoices must be submitted electronically in the XRechnung format.
    3. The fees are fixed until 31 December 2027. Thereafter the Provider may adjust the fees once per calendar year with three months' notice, at most to the extent of the change in the index of negotiated wages for the information and communication sector published by the Federal Statistical Office.
    4. Service Credits are deducted from the invoice for the month following the month in which the Service Level was missed.

  9. Data protection and information security

    1. Insofar as the Provider processes personal data on behalf of the Customer, the data processing agreement in Annex C applies.
    2. The Provider shall maintain an information security management system certified to ISO/IEC 27001 throughout the term of this Agreement and shall present the current certificate on request.
    3. The Provider shall notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any case within 24 hours, of any security incident affecting Customer Data or the Services, and shall support the Customer in meeting its notification obligations.
    4. The Customer may audit compliance with this clause once per calendar year, or at any time for cause, with fourteen days' notice. The Provider may meet audit requests by presenting current certificates and audit reports of independent auditors where these cover the subject matter of the audit.

  10. Confidentiality

    1. Each Party shall keep confidential all information of the other Party that is marked as confidential or whose confidential nature is evident from the circumstances, and shall use it only for the purposes of this Agreement.
    2. The obligation does not apply to information that was demonstrably known to the receiving Party before disclosure, that is or becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement, or that must be disclosed by law or by order of a court or authority.
    3. The confidentiality obligation survives the termination of this Agreement for a period of five years.

  11. Rights of use

    1. The Provider grants the Customer a non-exclusive right, unlimited in time and space, to use all documentation, scripts and configurations created specifically for the Customer within the scope of the Services.
    2. Rights to the Provider's standard tools and pre-existing materials remain with the Provider. The Customer receives a right to use them for the term of this Agreement and the exit phase.

  12. Liability

    1. The Parties are liable without limitation for damage caused wilfully or by gross negligence, for injury to life, body or health and under the Product Liability Act.
    2. In the event of slightly negligent breach of a material contractual obligation, liability is limited to the foreseeable damage typical for this type of contract, but to no more than 150 percent of the annual fees in the contract year in which the damage occurred.
    3. Liability for loss of data is limited to the cost of restoring the data from backups that the Provider is required to create under Annex A.
    4. The Provider shall maintain professional liability insurance with a cover of at least EUR 5 million per claim throughout the term of this Agreement.

  13. Term

    1. This Agreement enters into force upon signature by both Parties. The term of the Services begins on 1 January 2027 and ends on 31 December 2029.
    2. The Customer may extend the term twice by twelve months each by written notice given at least six months before the end of the current term.

  14. Termination

    1. The right of either Party to terminate this Agreement for cause remains unaffected. Cause exists for the Customer in particular if the Provider misses the availability Service Level of a service of category A in three consecutive months, or if the Provider's ISO/IEC 27001 certification is withdrawn.
    2. Termination must be in writing to be effective.

  15. Exit management

    1. Upon termination of this Agreement, for whatever reason, the Provider shall support the Customer or a successor nominated by the Customer in taking over the Services for a period of up to six months (exit phase) and shall continue to provide the Services during this period at the agreed fees.
    2. The Provider shall hand over all Customer Data in a commonly used, machine-readable format and all documentation required for operation, and shall delete Customer Data after confirmation of successful handover, with the exception of data it is legally required to retain.
    3. The Provider shall update the exit plan annually and present it to the Customer by 31 March of each year.

  16. Final provisions

    1. Amendments and supplements to this Agreement, including this clause, must be made in writing. Text form is sufficient for change requests under clause 2.4.
    2. Should any provision of this Agreement be or become invalid, the validity of the remaining provisions remains unaffected. The Parties shall replace the invalid provision with a valid provision that comes as close as possible to the economic purpose of the invalid provision.
    3. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Germany, excluding the UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods. The place of jurisdiction is Hamburg.

The Provider processes personal data on behalf of the Customer within the meaning of Art. 28 GDPR for the duration of the Agreement and the exit phase. The Provider processes personal data only on documented instructions from the Customer, unless required to do so by Union or Member State law; in such a case, the Provider shall inform the Customer of that legal requirement before processing, unless that law prohibits such information.

C.5 Rights and obligations

The Provider shall support the Customer with appropriate technical and organisational measures in responding to requests from data subjects, in carrying out data protection impact assessments and in consultations with the supervisory authority. After the end of the provision of services, the Provider shall, at the choice of the Customer, delete or return all personal data and delete existing copies unless Union or Member State law requires storage of the personal data. The Provider shall make available to the Customer all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with the obligations laid down in Art. 28 GDPR and allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by the Customer or another auditor mandated by the Customer.

The Provider shall inform the Customer immediately if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes the GDPR or other Union or Member State data protection provisions. The Provider shall ensure that persons authorised to process the personal data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.
